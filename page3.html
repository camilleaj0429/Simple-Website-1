The Origin Story

Reviving an American automotive icon for the modern electric age.

The Scout legacy began decades ago as a rugged utility vehicle designed to conquer the toughest terrain and tackle everyday work with ease. Rooted in that pioneering spirit, Scout Motors was established to reimagine these legendary all-terrain capabilities through cutting-edge, sustainable electric vehicle engineering.

Our founders and engineering groups came together with a shared ambition: to build durable, versatile vehicles like the Traveler SUV and Terra truck that honor classic design while integrating next-generation technology. From meticulous data verification to robust system architecture, every vehicle is built to prepare drivers for the journey ahead.

What powertrain options are available?

Scout vehicles offer both all-electric configurations and the Harvester range-extender option to support extended driving ranges.

How can I reserve a Scout Traveler or Terra?

Reservations can be placed securely online by visiting the official Scout Motors website and selecting your preferred configuration.

Where are Scout vehicles manufactured?

Scout vehicles are built in the United States at our dedicated production facility located in Blythewood, South Carolina.
